# BIS Certification Process — Food Products

Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-overview/

## Scope
Unlike electronics (which fall under CRS), most food products that carry
BIS certification go through BIS's general **Product Certification** /
**FMCS (Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme)**-adjacent process
rather than CRS, since food is largely outside CRS scope.

## Key points for the assistant
- FMCS covers products with Indian Standards **except** Electronics & IT
  (which fall under CRS instead).
- For any food item under a notified QCO, the exact QCO text should be
  retrieved and quoted (marking requirements, applicability, effective date).
- For non-QCO food items with only a voluntary IS standard, the assistant
  should clarify that certification is optional and note FSSAI's mandatory
  requirements as the actual compliance path.
