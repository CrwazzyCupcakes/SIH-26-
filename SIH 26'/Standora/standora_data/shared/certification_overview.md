# General BIS Certification Process — Overview

Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-overview/

## Key concepts for the assistant to distinguish

- **QCO (Quality Control Order):** a notification that makes BIS
  certification mandatory for a specific product category. If no QCO
  exists for a product, BIS certification is generally voluntary.
- **Standard Mark under Licence:** the traditional ISI-mark licensing route
  (used for toys, household appliances, etc.) — involves factory inspection.
- **CoC (Certificate of Conformity):** an alternative certification route
  under some schemes, generally lighter-weight than full licensing.
- **FMCS (Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme):** covers products
  with Indian Standards **except** Electronics & IT (which fall under CRS
  instead). Relevant for foreign manufacturers seeking BIS certification.
- **CRS (Compulsory Registration Scheme):** specific to Electronics & IT
  goods — registration-based rather than full licensing (see
  electricals/certification_process.md for the detailed CRS flow).

## Routing logic for the assistant
When a user asks "how do I get my product BIS certified," the assistant
should first determine:
1. Is this an Electronics/IT product? → CRS route.
2. Is this covered by a QCO in another category (e.g., toys)? → Scheme-I
   licensing route.
3. Is there no QCO, but a voluntary IS standard exists? → optional FMCS-style
   certification for market credibility, not mandatory.
