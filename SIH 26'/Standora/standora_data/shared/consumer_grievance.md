# Consumer Complaint / Grievance Process

Source: https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/nch

## Channels available to consumers

- **eBIS / Standard Promotion Portal** — online complaint registration.
- **BIS CARE app** — mobile app for lodging complaints and verifying
  certification marks.
- **Email** — complaints@bis.gov.in
- **Nearest BIS regional office** — in-person/postal complaints.
- **National Consumer Helpline** — 1915 (toll-free), also available via
  WhatsApp, SMS, app, and web portal, for broader consumer issues beyond
  BIS-specific certification complaints.

## Notes for the assistant
- If a user's query is about a certification mark that looks fake, or a
  product that doesn't perform as certified, this is the routing path —
  distinct from general BIS knowledge/standards questions.
- Distinguish BIS-specific channels (CARE app, eBIS) from the broader
  National Consumer Helpline, which handles all consumer issues, not just
  standards/certification.
