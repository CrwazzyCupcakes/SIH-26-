## What hallmarking is
Hallmarking certifies that a piece of jewellery conforms to the purity
standard laid down by BIS. It applies to gold and silver jewellery/artefacts.

## Key components
- **Jeweller registration** — jewellers must register with BIS (instant,
  lifetime registration model).
- **A&H Centre recognition** — Assaying & Hallmarking (A&H) centres are
  recognized under IS 15820:2009.
- **Process:** jeweller registers with BIS → submits jewellery to a
  BIS-recognized A&H centre → centre assays purity and applies the hallmark.
- **Consumer testing options** — consumers can get jewellery independently
  tested for purity verification at recognized centres.
