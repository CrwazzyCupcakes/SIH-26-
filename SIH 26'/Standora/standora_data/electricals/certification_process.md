# CRS Certification Process — Electrical & Electronics Products

Source: https://www.crsbis.in/BIS/registration-page.do
Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-overview/

## Scope
This document covers the registration process under the Compulsory Registration
Scheme (CRS) for electronics and IT goods, as required under Scheme-II of BIS.

## Process Steps

1. **Create an account** on the CRS portal (crsbis.in).
2. **Generate a test request** for the specific product/model.
3. **Get the product tested** at a BIS-recognized laboratory.
4. **Verify the test report** once issued by the lab.
5. **Apply for registration** within 90 days of the test report date.
6. **Submit required checklist documents** along with the application.
7. **Grant of registration** — BIS issues an R-number upon approval.
8. **Marking** — the registered product must bear the Standard Mark/QR code
   linked to the R-number.

## Notes for the assistant
- A consumer can verify a product's registration validity by checking the
  R-number against the BIS portal.
- Manufacturers/importers must register per model, not just per brand.
- Registration must be renewed/kept valid; an expired R-number means the
  product is no longer compliant even if previously certified.
