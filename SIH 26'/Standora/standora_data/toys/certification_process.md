# BIS Licensing Process — Toys (Scheme-I)

Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Toy_QC_order.pdf

## Process Steps

1. **Identify applicable IS 9873 parts** for the specific toy type (mechanical,
   flammability, chemical, phthalates, etc. depending on toy category).
2. **Testing** at a BIS-recognized toy-safety laboratory.
3. **Apply for BIS licence** under Scheme-I of Schedule-II of the BIS
   (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
4. **Factory inspection** (as applicable, per BIS assessment).
5. **Grant of licence** — manufacturer can now use the ISI mark on toys.

## Notes for the assistant
- This differs from the electronics CRS process (registration-based) —
  toys go through a **licensing** scheme (Scheme-I), which is a heavier
  process involving factory inspection, not just registration.
- Product Manual PM/9873 documents (2023-2024) contain sampling and test
  method details — useful for detailed guidance once fully sourced.
