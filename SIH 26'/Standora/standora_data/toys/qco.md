# Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020

Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Toy_QC_order.pdf
Source: https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Safety_of_toys.pdf

## Summary

The Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020 mandates that toys conform to the
IS 9873 series of standards and requires BIS licensing under Scheme-I of
Schedule-II of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.

- **Effective date:** 1 January 2021.
- **Applicability:** Manufacturers and importers of toys intended for
  children under 14 years of age.
- **Requirement:** Toys must conform to applicable IS 9873 parts (mechanical/
  physical, flammability, chemical, and other relevant parts depending on
  toy type) and must bear the BIS Standard Mark (ISI mark) before sale.

## Certification/licensing path (MSME-relevant)

1. Identify which IS 9873 parts apply to the specific toy type.
2. Get the toy tested at a BIS-recognized toy-safety testing lab.
3. Apply for a BIS licence under Scheme-I.
4. Factory inspection (as applicable).
5. Licence granted — toy can bear the ISI mark.

## Notes for the assistant
- This is one of the clearest MSME pain points: many small toy
  manufacturers are unaware certification became mandatory in 2021.
- Product Manual (PM/9873) documents provide sampling rules, test methods,
  and "series" grouping rules for testing — useful for detailed clause-level
  answers once sourced in full.
